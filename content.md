## Training Categories

**Personal Training**
One-on-one sessions with a certified trainer, built around your goals — strength, weight loss, mobility, or sport-specific performance. Includes a starting assessment and a plan that adjusts as you progress.

**Group Fitness Classes**
High-energy, instructor-led classes for every level — HIIT, strength circuits, and functional conditioning. No sign-up fees, no class caps hidden behind a higher tier — if it's on the schedule, it's included.

**Functional Training**
Movement-based training using kettlebells, sleds, battle ropes, and bodyweight work — built for real-world strength and conditioning, not just machine reps.
